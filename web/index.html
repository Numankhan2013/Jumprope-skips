How this is worked out
  • Intensity comes from your measured pace: rope jumping is 8.8 MET under 100 skips/min, 11.8 at 100–120, and 12.3 at 120–160 (Compendium of Physical Activities).
  • One MET is set to your resting metabolism from weight, height, age and sex (Mifflin-St Jeor), not a textbook average.
  • Rest time between sets counts at 2 MET. Any MET-based estimate is about ±15% for one person, hence the range.

Already know your count?

How it counts. Tracks how the moving part of the picture (you) goes up and down from frame to frame, finds the rhythm of your jumps, and counts one skip per bounce. Pauses, the swing that starts a set, the hop where the rope catches your feet, and moments when the phone itself moves are left out.

Processing speed depends on your phone; expect roughly a third to a half of the video's length. Keep this screen open while it works.
